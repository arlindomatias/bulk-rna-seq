Study design

GEO GSE295281 (BioProject PRJNA1254109): hippocampus of 12-week-old female C57BL/6J mice, one month after AC-T chemotherapy (doxorubicin + cyclophosphamide, then paclitaxel) or saline. Paired-end RNA-seq on an Illumina NextSeq 2000.

  • Saline: 6 mice.
  • AC-T: 5 mice.

This project stops at the count table. Differential expression is done in bulk_rna_seq_tximport.

Pipeline

  1. Download. .sra files come from the NCBI mirror on AWS and are checked with vdb-validate. fasterq-dump writes temporary FASTQ files to a local SSD. They are deleted after FastQC and Salmon.
  2. Read QC. FastQC runs on each read, and MultiQC collects FastQC and Salmon (MultiQC report).
  3. Reference. GENCODE mouse M35 transcriptome (GRCm39). The Salmon index uses -k 31 --gencode, transcriptome only (no decoys).
  4. Quantification. salmon quant -l A --gcBias --seqBias. A sample fails if its mapping rate is below 30%. Every sample uses the same Salmon version and index.
  5. Gene level. tximport summarizes transcripts to genes and writes counts_gene.tsv, tpm_gene.tsv, counts_gene_lengthScaledTPM.tsv, length_gene.tsv and data/txi_gene.rds (input of the DE project).

Large files (SRA, reference, index, Salmon outputs) are stored under $DATA/GSE295281/.

Raw read quality (FastQC)

FastQC flags compare each file with a generic genomic library. Several flags are expected for RNA-seq and do not mean the data are poor.

  • Sequence duplication fails in 22 of 22 files. This is expected in RNA-seq: highly expressed transcripts produce many identical reads.
  • Per base sequence content warns or fails at the read start. Random-hexamer priming causes this bias at the first bases of every RNA-seq library.
  • Per tile sequence quality fails in every file. NextSeq flow cells often trigger this module.
  • Per base sequence quality fails in 11 files. In the plot below, mean quality stays above Q30 along the read and drops only at the last cycle of read 2. Salmon's selective alignment is not affected by one low-quality base, so no trimming was applied.
  • GC content, N content and overrepresented sequences fail mostly in read 2.

Quantification (Salmon)

All samples were quantified with Salmon 2.11.0. The library type detected automatically was ISR in every sample (stranded, read 1 from the reverse strand). Mapping rates ranged from 91.6% to 93.5%. The number of pairs Salmon processed matches the count reported by ENA in every sample.

Gene count table

tximport gives 56,065 genes × 11 samples. Of these, 16,805 genes have at least 10 counts in every sample.

Read composition

Protein-coding genes take 83–87% of the reads and mitochondrial genes 10–15%. rRNA genes take at most 0.18% of the reads.

Detected and most abundant genes

12 of the 20 most abundant genes are mitochondrial. The others include Mbp, Gm29216, Kif5a, Camk2a, Plp1, Snap25.

Sample similarity

Correlation between samples ranges from 0.984 to 0.997. No sample is a clear outlier. The PCA here is only an overview: the DE project repeats it on variance-stabilized counts after filtering.
